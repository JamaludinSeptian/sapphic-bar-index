About the project

The Sapphic Bar Index is an independent field guide to sapphic bars, spaces, and nights out in Barcelona. It ranks venues with a 0–100 score and short field notes, aiming to help women-loving women find places where they can feel at home, meet people, and have a good time — rather than walking into yet another bar aimed squarely at men.

This page is a study reconstruction of that guide, rebuilt from scratch for learning purposes. The listings and scores above are reproduced from the original; the design, code, fonts, and artwork here are substitutes made for this exercise.

For the real, living index — and to submit a spot of your own — visit sapphicindex.com.
